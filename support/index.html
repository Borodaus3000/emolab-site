Subscription

  • Cancel or change a subscription: in the app, Settings → "Manage subscription", or in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases on a new phone or after reinstalling: open the Premium screen and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you bought the subscription with.

Your data

  • Data about your partner and her cycle is stored only on your phone. There is no sign-up and no cloud copy.
  • Delete a partner: on her screen, tap the pencil → "Delete persona".
  • Delete all data: Settings → "Delete all my data". This does not cancel your subscription.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Not a medical tool

EmoLab is a relationship helper. The forecast is approximate: do not use it for contraception, pregnancy planning or diagnosis.
