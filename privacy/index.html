EmoLab Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

EmoLab helps you be more attentive to your partner: from the dates of her period it builds a forecast of cycle phases, shows daily tips and reminds you when a phase changes. This page explains in plain words what data the app keeps, why, and who it is shared with.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The developer and data controller is Kirill Sibiriakov, a private individual. Email for any data questions: sibiriakov@icloud.com.

2. In short

  • Data about your partner and her cycle is stored only on your phone and is never sent anywhere.
  • There is no sign-up: no email, password or phone number. You are signed in anonymously.
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell data or share it for advertising or marketing.
  • Cycle data is used only to make the app work: the forecast, tips and reminders.

3. What data is kept

What you enter about your partner:

  • her name (or any other name you choose);
  • her age, if you enter it;
  • the goal you chose: "Be her support every day", "Plan dates better" or "Just understand her better";
  • the period dates you mark;
  • notification settings: whether the "Every day" notification is on, which phases to remind you about, how many days before, and at what time.

What the app calculates: the cycle phase forecast, expected period dates and fertile window, average cycle and period length.

"How is she?" marks (for example, "calm" or "tired") stay only on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

Technical data:

  • an anonymous user ID, created on first launch;
  • purchase data: which plan you have, trial status and subscription period.

4. Why

  • To build the phase forecast and show a tip for each day.
  • To send notifications, only if you turned them on: "Every day" about where she is in her cycle today, with a tip of the day, and reminders about phase changes.
  • To process and restore your subscription.

Cycle data is not used for advertising, not sold and not shared with data brokers.

5. Where data is stored and who processes it

  • Your phone. Everything is stored here: the partner profile, period dates, the forecast, "How is she?" marks and reminder settings.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): anonymous sign-in. Firebase only keeps an anonymous session identifier; your partner's and cycle data is not sent there.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): subscription processing. It receives your anonymous ID, purchase history, and a few service labels about your first time using the app: which goal you chose; whether period dates were entered, and whether you skipped that step on first launch; how far you got through the first-run steps; where the subscription screen was opened from and whether you saw it. This helps us understand where people stop during the first run. RevenueCat does not receive your partner's name or cycle dates.
  • Apple: App Store payments.

These companies process data on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. Data may be stored and processed outside your country, including in the USA.

6. Notifications

Notifications are created on your phone. The title is your partner's name and the text is a forecast based on her cycle: for example, "The forecast says a period may start tomorrow" in a phase reminder, or "In 3 days — the period" and a tip of the day in the "Every day" notification. These notifications can be visible on the lock screen. You can hide their content in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Show Previews; the "Every day" notification then shows "Tip of the day" instead of its text. You can turn notifications off at any time, in the app or in your phone's settings.

7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data is stored on your phone while you use the app.

  • Delete a partner: on her screen, tap the pencil → "Delete persona". After confirmation, her profile, period dates, forecast, "How is she?" marks and reminder settings are erased on the phone.
  • Delete all data: Settings → "Delete all my data". All data on the phone is erased permanently. This does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in App Store settings.
  • Deleting the app erases all data on the phone. RevenueCat keeps your anonymous ID and purchase history so your subscription keeps working regardless of the data on your phone.

If something does not work, email sibiriakov@icloud.com.

8. Data about another person

You enter data about your partner, who is another person. Please do so with her knowledge. The app never contacts her and never shares your records with her.

9. Your rights

You can find out what data is kept about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent to notifications. Most of this can be done right in the app; for the rest, email sibiriakov@icloud.com. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority of your country.

10. Security

Data about your partner and her cycle is stored only on your phone and is never shared except as described in this document. Network requests (anonymous sign-in, subscription processing) use an encrypted connection. No protection is absolute, but we keep only the minimum data the app needs.

11. Children

The app is not intended for people under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Not a medical tool

EmoLab is a relationship helper, not a medical app. The forecast is approximate: do not use it for contraception, pregnancy planning or diagnosis.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of the page and tell you about significant changes in the app.
